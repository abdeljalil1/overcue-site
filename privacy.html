Privacy Policy

Overcue for Mac · Last updated November 10, 2026

What Overcue collects

Nothing. Overcue does not collect, upload, sell, or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising code, and no tracking.

What stays on your Mac

Your scripts and settings are saved only on your Mac, inside Overcue's private storage, and are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes them.

Microphone and speech recognition

Only when you turn on Voice Follow, Overcue listens through your microphone so the script can scroll as you speak. Speech is recognized by macOS on your Mac (on-device recognition): the audio is not recorded, not saved, and not sent to Overcue, to Apple, or to anyone else. Overcue only uses the recognized words to find your place in the script, and forgets them right away. While it listens, the prompter shows “Listening” and macOS shows its microphone indicator.

Purchases

Overcue Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; Overcue only learns whether Pro is unlocked, never your name or payment details.

Children

Overcue does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
